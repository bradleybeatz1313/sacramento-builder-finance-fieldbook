The builder finance desk

Make the sales team safer when financing gets messy.

Builders do not need a product catalog. They need an operator who catches risk before a cancellation, owns escalation and communicates without drama.

01

Rapid triage

Buyer consultation, document checklist and a realistic next-step path during model-home hours.

02

Second-look rescue

Complex income, FHA/VA, jumbo, non-QM, sale-of-home and other scenarios the first lane cannot solve.

03

Payment intelligence

Community-specific costs including HOA, special assessments, taxes, solar and cash to close—not rate alone.

04

Milestone control

Buyer-authorized status reporting, one escalation owner and no surprise at appraisal, lock or closing.

Ready-to-use language

Ask for the problem, not the referral.

EMAIL · SALES LEADER
Hi [Name]—I’m a Sacramento-area loan officer. I’m not contacting you to disrupt an existing preferred-lender relationship. I help builder teams with buyers who need a second financing path: decline rescues, complex income, FHA/VA/jumbo scenarios, sale-of-home complications or simply faster escalation before a contract is lost. Could I spend 15 minutes learning where financing fallout occurs in your current process?

Execution cadence

90 days from research to repeatable applications.

Activity targets are operating goals, not forecasts. Track service and conversion—not referral-linked economics.

Conservative operating policy · RESPA + company rules

Create value for buyers.
Never exchange value for referrals.

Never

  • Pay per lead, application, approval, closing or volume
  • Reward builder employees with gifts, meals or services for referrals
  • Tie benefits to capture rate, exclusivity or production
  • Provide free ordinary builder operations
  • Disguise a warm handoff as “marketing”

Approval required

  • Builder, preferred or backup-lender agreements
  • Co-branded assets and payment illustrations
  • Desk rent, office hours and data sharing
  • Sponsorships, shared ads and MSAs
  • Incentives, gifts or any in-kind value

Lower-risk posture

  • Broad educational events not conditioned on referrals
  • Documented fair-market advertising actually delivered
  • Neutral lender choice and buyer consent
  • Service metrics unrelated to referral compensation
  • Official Loan Estimates for cost comparison
Not legal advice. The headline above is a deliberately conservative operating rule, not a complete statement of RESPA. Regulation X contains defined scope, exceptions and conditions. Every arrangement must follow applicable law and the loan officer’s current company policy after compliance/legal review. Review Regulation X §1024.14 and §1024.15.
